\begin{abstract}
A small server, having been much visited in its historical particulars,
petitions for a little proportion. The quantities in the Schedule are real;
the Court, its jurisdiction, its fees, and its magnificent opinion of
itself are wholly invented. The only practical request is the courteous
forwarding of a message to the relevant customer, where feasible.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section*{The Petition}
Petitioner comes now, entirely without counsel and somewhat without sleep,
to set forth a grievance of such limited terrestrial importance that it
has become necessary to furnish it with an elaborate title.
\note{The Court is self-appointed. Its appointment has not improved its prospects.}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textsc{The Premises.} Petitioner maintains one (1) modest virtual
server, including a public Forgejo instance at
\url{https://git.levineuwirth.org}. Capitalizing ``Premises'' shall not
be construed as making the server larger.
\item \textsc{The Visitors.} Certain persons or processes unknown have
demonstrated an interest in the repository's earlier editions exceeding
that ordinarily exhibited by their author. Some mistakes had already
been corrected. Their continued popularity is therefore personal.
\item \textsc{The Addressee.} This courtesy petition is addressed to
EGIHosting's network operations or abuse desk because one observed source
allocation was registered to EGIHosting. The registry entry identifies
infrastructure; it does not identify the crawler's customer or establish
that EGIHosting directed the requests.
\item \textsc{The Custody of Old Mistakes.} A separate, server-wide
maintenance rehearsal disclosed 2,464 queued source-archive jobs.
No finding allocates that queue to this addressee. Petitioner merely
observes that his past errors had acquired a distribution department.
\item \textsc{The Proceedings Below.} The server installed Anubis,
raised its computational admission charge twice, and continued keeping
records. This is the ordinary administration of a website, now dressed
as a constitutional crisis.
\end{enumerate}

\note{No customer identity is sought. No finding concerning training use is made.}
The Court's jurisdiction extends to the author's choice of page margins,
his robots policy, and the expression of mild astonishment. It extends
no further. Venue is proper wherever this PDF has been unnecessarily opened.

\leaf{The Prayer for Symbolic Relief}{prayer}
Wherefore Petitioner respectfully asks, without attempting to manufacture
an obligation by the accumulation of capital letters, that the following
modest relief be contemplated.
\note{No injunction is requested. A voluntary moment of reflection would suffice.}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textsc{Courtesy forwarding.} If feasible, convey the accompanying
message to the customer responsible for the relevant automated traffic.
No disclosure of its name, billing records, or other identifying details
is requested.
\item \textsc{An inventory of enthusiasm.} Invite that customer to ask
whether it has already obtained the material it presently proposes to
obtain again, and whether the answer could be checked before another
archaeological expedition is dispatched.
\item \textsc{A reconsideration of scale.} Invite a little restraint in
requesting historical pages from a small public forge. The author is
pleased to be read; he had not understood this to require a logistics sector.
\item \textsc{Compensation in the proper currency.} Award the Petitioner
three imaginary gold pieces and one entirely hypothetical biscuit,
payable solely in metaphor. Monetary amount due: zero. No account shall
be opened, no invoice raised, and no biscuit pursued through collections.
\item \textsc{Disposition.} Accept a brief acknowledgment, an amused
forwarding, or silence as the recipient's convenience permits. Silence
creates no admission, default judgment, or sequel fee. It may, however,
fail to discourage the author from writing a sequel.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Stipulation concerning the assistance of humans}
Petitioner recognizes that the person reading an abuse mailbox is
probably not the person who commissioned the crawl. No grievance is
advanced against that person's afternoon. The attachments have therefore
been limited to a completely unnecessary petition and the facts needed
to understand why it exists.
\note{Clerks are to be treated kindly. They have already received the PDF.}
