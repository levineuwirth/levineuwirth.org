Every server on the internet is knocked on all day. This one takes no passwords, so none of these attempts can succeed; they are tallied here beside the [Crawler Chronicles](index.html).

A network named here is where the connections came from, usually a rented server. It says whose addresses were used, not who used them.

<!-- ledger:totals -->
From October 17, 2026 to November 18, 2026: **108,801** connections tried a username, from 4,243 addresses in 1,095 networks.
<!-- /ledger:totals -->

## The networks

Networks are ranked by the connections that tried a username, and named only when at least three of their addresses tried one. Research scanners, which connect without trying any name, are left out.

## About these numbers

A *try* is one connection that sent at least a username; an *address* is counted once however often it returned. Logins of the server's own users are not counted, and no address is shown. The ledger is refreshed about monthly by hand.

<!-- ledger:coverage -->
- The period is whole days in UTC, October 17, 2026 to November 18, 2026.
- Until October 23, 2026 only the server's own SSH log was read: attempts on the git server's SSH, and connections that failed before a handshake, count from that day.
- The records begin at 16:00 UTC on October 17, 2026.
- Networks are origin ASes in the iptoasn.com table of November 19, 2026; the country is the AS's registration, not where an address is.
- Snapshot taken November 19, 2026.
