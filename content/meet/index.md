If something in my [work](/work.html) has left you with a question, an
objection, or an idea worth pursuing together, I would be glad to hear from you.

Write to [ln@levineuwirth.org](mailto:ln@levineuwirth.org). A few sentences about
what you have been reading and what you would like to discuss are a good
beginning. A link to a paper, project, or particular passage is welcome.

## Research conversations

Some questions are easier to work through in conversation. If you would like
to talk about verifiable inference, model evaluation, Proof Broker, or a
possible collaboration, mention that in your email and we can arrange a time.
A focused half-hour is a useful starting point; an ongoing discussion may
need longer.

I am based in Copenhagen. When suggesting a time, please include your time
zone so that we can account for the difference.

## Letters

There is no need to arrange a meeting to reach me. Questions, corrections,
and longer thoughts are welcome by email, including those that do not call
for a reply in real time.
